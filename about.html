How It All Started

Some lessons cannot be taught. They have to be lived, built and felt — in the nerves before a premiere, the excitement of seeing a project come together, and the applause at the end.

Some of our most meaningful learning experiences have happened alongside young people, through projects that pushed us out of our comfort zones and brought us closer together. That is how the first Zoom In Short Film Festival came about — from courage, determination and a desire to do something that could make a difference.

Together with the IBSB students volunteering with the “Adoptă o Casă” programme, we wanted to create more than a school event. We wanted a charity film festival open to young people from across Bucharest. We wanted to support the “Adoptă o Casă” programme in Roșia Montană, which has spent 15 years helping to preserve the area’s built heritage. And we wanted to hold the festival somewhere with a story of its own: Cinema Europa, Bucharest’s last remaining neighbourhood cinema, which is currently undergoing an exciting process of revitalisation.

Getting there was intense, challenging and very real. There were Sunday mornings at 7 a.m. in the cinema, working on the festival’s visual identity, promotion, sponsorships, media partnerships, contracts, GDPR, automations and hundreds of messages. There were difficult decisions, unexpected problems and solutions we had to find along the way. Most of all, there was an extraordinary level of commitment.

And the students did it not because they needed another line on their CVs. Most of them already had excellent opportunities ahead of them. They did it because they wanted to create something that mattered to someone beyond themselves.

The idea itself was born from a setback. The previous year, we had tried to organise a film competition within our school, and it simply didn’t work. This time, we decided to open the doors wider. We thought perhaps 30 young people might enter. In the end, there were five.

And you know what? Five was enough.

Because every film took courage. Every young filmmaker had a voice. And we learned that success is not always measured in numbers. Sometimes it is measured by meaning.

We are deeply grateful to everyone who supported us and encouraged us when we were beginning to doubt ourselves: Claudia Apostol, Andreea Duminicioiu, Cristiana Dumitru and the Cinema Europa team. We are grateful to our sponsors, Catena, UniCredit and HBO; our media partners, Euronews, ProTV and Frame Film; our jury, Corina Șuteu, Paula Antonia Cămîrzan and Mihai Mănescu; and Silvia Dragic and Tangaj Productions.

Most of all, we are grateful to the young people who had the courage to submit their films and to everyone who came to watch them.

Zoom In was more than a festival. It showed us what can happen when a team believes in an idea — and in a cause bigger than itself.

And that was just the beginning.

We are already preparing for the second edition, with even more enthusiasm for what comes next.

The "Zoom In" Philosophy

Our mission is built on the belief that the cinematic lens can do more than just record history—it can act as the catalyst for its restoration. We organize our vision around three core pillars:
Zoom In on the Screen

ZIFF gives young filmmakers a space to share their stories and explore different ways of seeing the world. We welcome films that reflect the experiences, ideas and concerns of a new generation of storytellers, including documentary and realistic approaches.
Through film, we want to turn places, people and stories that might otherwise be overlooked into something audiences can connect with — from the dust and stones of historic landscapes, to the stories of the communities that call them home.

Zoom In on the Heritage

We want to connect what happens on the cinema screen with the real work of preserving Roșia Montană’s historic buildings. The festival helps raise funds for the “Adoptă o Casă” programme, supporting restoration work carried out using traditional techniques and local craftsmanship, including lime-sand plastering, stone masonry and wooden shingle roofing. This helps preserve the buildings in a way that respects their history and original character.

Zoom In on the Community

ZIFF brings together young filmmakers, audiences and people working to preserve the heritage of Roșia Montană. We believe that film can do more than tell stories — it can start conversations, bring people together and inspire us to care about the places and communities around us.
By connecting creative young people with the local community and the craftspeople who are helping to restore Roșia Montană, we hope to support a future in which its history, traditions and unique character can continue to be valued and shared.

Our Impact & Legacy

Festival Executive Summary

Organized by Year 12 and 13 student volunteers from the International British School of Bucharest, the Zoom-In Film Festival 2026 combined youth artistic showcase with meaningful social impact. Hosted at Cinematograful Europa, the event featured a dual mission: supporting the Loc cu Loc cinema renovation campaign and fundraising for the ARA Association’s Adoptă o Casă la Roșia Montană program.

  • Audience & Platform: Brought together ~100 in-person attendees and reached 2,130+ unique web visitors, giving high school filmmakers from Bucharest and Brașov a professional big-screen experience.
  • Industry Expert Evaluation: Features five competing student short films judged by a distinguished panel including film critic Corina Șuteu, producer Paula Antonia Tudor-Cămîrzan, and screenwriter Mihai Mănescu, combined with real-time electronic audience voting.
  • Strategic Sponsor Partnerships: Secured major corporate sponsorships and pro bono contributions from partners including Spring Farma, UniCredit Bank, HBO, Catena, and Frame Film. Sponsorships fully funded equipment vouchers, SKVOT directing courses, media internships, and all operational expenses.
  • Direct Philanthropic Yield: Operating with complete cost efficiency, 100% of collected participation fees were directed straight to ARA’s historic preservation projects in Roșia Montană.
