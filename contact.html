Contact ZIFF

ZIFF brings young people together through film while supporting the preservation of Roșia Montană’s heritage.
Whether you’re a filmmaker interested in taking part, a potential partner who would like to support the festival and its cause, or a journalist looking for more information, we’d love to hear from you.
Get in touch to find out more about ZIFF, our festival, and the “Adoptă o Casă” programme we support.

Contacts

1. General & Submission Inquiries
For information regarding the 2027 Call for Entries, technical specifications for "engaged cinema," or general questions about our upcoming programming in Bucharest:
Email: contact@zoomin-filmfest.com
Subject: General Inquiry / Submissions – [Your Name/Film Title]
Note: Submissions are not yet open. Please follow our digital channels for the official announcement.

2. Press & Media Relations
For media accreditation, requests for our Brand Kit, or to schedule interviews with our student founders—Marc Paraschiv and Theodor Aizic—and our professional mentor, Johanna Croci:
Email: press@zoomin-filmfest.com
Subject: Press Inquiry – [Organization Name]

3. Partnerships & Philanthropy
To discuss corporate social responsibility (CSR) goals, sponsorship opportunities, or how your contribution supports both the arts and the ARA Association’s restoration efforts in Roșia Montană:
• Email: partners@zoomin-filmfest.com
• Subject: Partnership Opportunity – ZIFF 2027

4. Integrity & Ethics Reporting
As an independent entity committed to international standards of excellence, we provide a confidential channel to report any concerns regarding fairness or professional conduct [Conversation History]:
• Email: ethics@zoomin-filmfest.com
• Subject: Fairness Report – Confidential

Stay Focused: Follow Our Channels
