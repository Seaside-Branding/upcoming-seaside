ZIFF 2027 Help Center & FAQ

Welcome to the Zoom in Film Festival (ZIFF) Help Center. As an independent cultural entity, we are committed to providing a transparent and supportive experience for our community of filmmakers, partners, and audience members. Whether you are looking for technical submission details or information on our collaborative philanthropic model, you will find the answers here.

FAQ

1. General Festival Information

ZIFF (Zoom In Film Festival) is a short film festival dedicated to young filmmakers aged 14–21. It provides a platform for emerging creators to share their stories, showcase their work, develop their filmmaking skills, and connect with others who share their passion for cinema. The festival also supports the protection of built cultural heritage through its partnership with the Adopt a House in Roșia Montană programme.

When and where will ZIFF 2027 take place?
ZIFF 2027 is scheduled to take place in February 2027 in Bucharest. Further details about the date, venue, and programme will be announced closer to the event.

Who is behind the festival?
ZIFF is a youth-led initiative organised by the Cadru cu Cadru Association, bringing together young volunteers with a shared interest in film, creativity, and cultural heritage. The team is supported by an initiative group including Johanna Croci, Theodor Aizic, and Marc Paraschiv, who provide guidance throughout the planning and delivery of the festival.

2. Film Submissions & Competition

Are submissions open? Yes! Submissions for ZIFF 2027 are now open. Young filmmakers aged 14–21 are invited to submit their short films and become part of the 2027 edition.

What types of films can I submit?
We welcome a wide range of short films, including fiction, documentary, animation, stop-motion, silent, and experimental films. All eligible films take part in the same competition and are evaluated according to the festival's official judging criteria.

Can I submit a film in Romanian?
Absolutely. Films can be in Romanian with English subtitles or English with Romanian subtitles. Subtitles should be clear, legible, and grammatically correct.

Who can enter the competition?
The competition is open to young filmmakers aged 14–21 who are enrolled in middle school, high school, or university. Teams can have up to five members, including actors, and each participant can be part of only one team.

How long should my film be?
Films must be between 7 and 10 minutes long, including credits.

Are there any content restrictions?
Yes. All films must be suitable for a teenage audience and comply with the festival's PG-13 content guidelines. Full details are available in the Official Regulations.

Do I need parental consent to participate?
If you are under 18, you will need written consent from a parent or legal representative to take part in the festival.

3. Donations & Supporting Our Cause

What cause does ZIFF support?
ZIFF is a charity event supporting the Adopt a House in Roșia Montană programme, initiated by the Architecture. Restoration. Archaeology (ARA) Association. The programme works to protect and rehabilitate the remarkable built cultural heritage of Roșia Montană.

How does the festival contribute?
Funds raised through festival registration fees are directed towards the Adopt a House in Roșia Montană programme, helping support its heritage conservation and restoration activities.

How can I support the cause?
You can support our mission by entering the festival, becoming a partner or sponsor, or making a donation. Details about available donation methods and ways to contribute are provided on our Donate Page.

4. Heritage Collaboration (ARA Association)

Is ZIFF part of the Adoptă o Casă la Roșia Montană programme?
No. ZIFF is organised independently by the Cadru cu Cadru Association and supports the Adoptă o Casă la Roșia Montană programme, initiated by the Arhitectură. Restaurare. Arheologie (ARA) Association.

How does ZIFF support the programme?
ZIFF supports the programme by raising funds through festival registration fees and by bringing greater visibility to the importance of protecting and preserving built cultural heritage. In this way, the festival connects the creativity and involvement of young people with meaningful heritage conservation efforts in Roșia Montană.

Why does a film festival support cultural heritage?
We believe that both film and built heritage preserve stories. By bringing the two together, ZIFF encourages young people not only to tell new stories through cinema, but also to recognise the value of protecting the places and histories that shape our shared cultural identity.

5. Ethics & Reporting 

How do you ensure a fair selection process?
All eligible films go through a pre-selection conducted by three evaluators appointed by the festival organisers, using the same core criteria as the final judging process. Selected films are then evaluated by a jury with relevant professional experience in film and media.

What standards of conduct are expected?
ZIFF is committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, and safe environment. All participants are expected to follow the principles of fair play, respect, ethics, and safety throughout the festival.

How can I report a concern?
If you experience or witness inappropriate conduct or have concerns about fairness, please visit our Integrity & Fairness page or contact us at contact@zoomin-filmfest.com. We encourage participants to raise concerns so they can be addressed appropriately and responsibly.
