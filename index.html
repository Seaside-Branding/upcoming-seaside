What is Zoom In?

The Zoom In Film Festival (ZIFF) is a student-led film festival that brings together creativity, community and a passion for protecting cultural heritage. More than just an opportunity to showcase short films made by young people, ZIFF helps raise awareness and funds for the “Adoptă o Casă” programme in Roșia Montană, supporting efforts to preserve the area’s historic buildings and local heritage.
By bringing young filmmakers and audiences together in Bucharest, the festival hopes to create a stronger connection between the city and the community of Roșia Montană — one that goes beyond the protests of 2013. Roșia Montană is a beautiful and diverse place, with a unique history that is worth protecting and sharing. There is still much work to be done to restore its historic buildings and help keep its heritage alive, and ZIFF is proud to support that effort through “Adoptă o Casă”.

How It All Started

Some lessons cannot be taught. They have to be lived, built and felt — in the nerves before a premiere, the excitement of seeing a project come together, and the applause at the end.

Some of our most meaningful learning experiences have happened alongside young people, through projects that pushed us out of our comfort zones and brought us closer together. That is how the first Zoom In Short Film Festival came about — from courage, determination and a desire to do something that could make a difference.

Meet the judges!

from ZIFF's 1st edition

Corina Șuteu

Corina Șuteu is an international cultural consultant and cultural policy expert with an international career spanning over 30 years in the arts and culture field. She was Minister of Culture in Romania and Director of the Romanian Cultural Institute in New York. Among many other major projects in various sectors of the arts, she founded two film festivals that she produces and whose president she is: Making Waves: New Romanian Cinema and fARAD, an international documentary film festival in Arad. Author of books on cultural management, author of articles in the magazine 22, Dilema, Observator Cultural and international magazines specialized in cultural policies, Corina Șuteu curates the space and programs of Insula 42 and works as an expert for various organizations in Romania and abroad.

Mihai Mănescu

Mihai Manescu is a screenwriter, author and communication specialist, PhD in Film and Media, founder of Sustin.org. He brings over a decade of teaching and storytelling expertise to our jury, blending hero’s journey insight with film industry and NGO experience.

Paula Tudor Cămîrzan

Paula Tudor Cămîrzan is a cultural manager and documentary film producer, vice president of the Antistatic Association and coordinator of the Via Danubiana project. She has international experience in documentary production and development, with studies in documentary film and visual anthropology.
