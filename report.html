Integrity & Fairness

ZIFF Ethics Reporting

The Zoom In Film Festival (ZIFF) is committed to creating a fair, respectful, inclusive, and safe environment for everyone involved in the festival.

As a youth-led initiative organised by the Cadru cu Cadru Association, we believe that creativity thrives in an environment built on respect, transparency, responsibility, and fair play. These principles guide not only our film competition, but also the way we work with participants, volunteers, partners, jury members, and audiences.

This page provides a dedicated space for members of the ZIFF community to raise concerns about unfair treatment, inappropriate behaviour, ethical issues, or other conduct that may conflict with the values and regulations of the festival.

Our Ethical Framework

Fair Competition:
All eligible films undergo a pre-selection conducted by three judges appointed by the festival organisers, using the same core criteria applied during the final judging process. Selected films are then evaluated by a jury with relevant experience in film and media.

Respectful and Safe Environment:
ZIFF does not tolerate abusive, discriminatory, intimidating, or dangerous behaviour. Everyone involved in the festival is expected to treat others with respect and to contribute to a safe and welcoming environment.

Responsible Support for Cultural Heritage:
ZIFF supports the Adoptă o Casă la Roșia Montană programme through funds raised from festival registration fees. We are committed to communicating clearly and responsibly about our charitable purpose and our collaboration with the Arhitectură. Restaurare. Arheologie. (ARA) Association.

What You Can Report?

    We encourage the reporting of any issues that compromise the integrity or values of the festival, including:

 unfair treatment or concerns about transparency in the film selection or judging process;

inappropriate, discriminatory, abusive, or unprofessional behaviour;

conduct that may compromise the safety or wellbeing of participants, volunteers, or visitors;

concerns about the responsible use of festival resources or the way our charitable and heritage partnerships are represented;

other conduct that may conflict with the Official Regulations or values of ZIFF.

Reporting and Resolution Process

All concerns will be treated seriously, sensitively, and with appropriate discretion. Reports will be reviewed by the festival's designated team, and appropriate steps will be taken based on the nature and circumstances of the concern.

You may choose to submit a report anonymously. However, providing as much relevant information as possible, including dates, circumstances, and any supporting information, will help us understand and appropriately address the issue.

Our aim is to ensure that everyone involved with ZIFF can participate in an environment where creativity, fairness, respect, and responsibility are valued and protected.

You may choose to remain anonymous. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us investigate the matter thoroughly.
