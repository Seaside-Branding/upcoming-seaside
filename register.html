Registrations

1. Film Categories

The Zoom In High School Short Film Festival welcomes young filmmakers working across a variety of styles, genres, and filmmaking techniques.

Eligible submissions include:

  • Short Fiction Films: live-action narrative films exploring original stories, themes, and creative perspectives.
  • Short Documentary Films: non-fiction works exploring real people, experiences, communities, events, or subjects.
  • Short Animated Films: films created using traditional animation, digital animation, stop-motion, or other animation techniques.
  • Experimental and Silent Films: creative works that explore alternative approaches to cinematic storytelling and visual expression.

All eligible films participate within the same competition and are evaluated according to the festival's official judging criteria.

2. Submission Guidelines & Eligibility

  • Age Requirement: The competition is open to young filmmakers aged 14 to 21, enrolled in middle school, high school, or university.
  • Team Structure: Each team may consist of a maximum of five members, including actors. Participants are free to distribute filmmaking roles, including directing, screenwriting, acting, cinematography, editing, and sound, within their team. A participant may not be part of more than one team during the same edition of the festival.
  • Runtime: Submitted films must have a total duration of 7 to 10 minutes, including credits. Films that do not meet the required duration may be subject to point deductions or may be excluded from the competition.
  • Language & Subtitles: Films may be submitted in Romanian, with English subtitles, or in English, with Romanian subtitles. All subtitles must be clear, legible, and grammatically correct.
  • Originality & Copyright: Submitted films must be the team's original work. Any external music, images, footage, or other copyrighted materials may only be used if they are royalty-free or if the team has obtained the copyright holder's written permission. All external materials must be appropriately credited.
  • Content Requirements: Films must be suitable for a teenage audience and comply with the festival's PG-13 content requirements outlined in the Official Regulations.

For participants under the age of 18, written consent from a parent or legal representative is mandatory.

3. Technical Specifications

All registrations must be completed through the official Zoom In Film Fest registration process.

Films must meet the following minimum technical requirements:

  • Format: MP4, MOV, or another compatible digital format approved by the organizers;
  • Resolution: Minimum 1080p;
  • Orientation: Landscape or portrait;
  • Sound: Clear and intelligible, without significant distortion.

Following registration, teams will receive instructions by email regarding the secure transfer of their film.

Registration is considered complete only after:

  • completing the official registration form;
  • confirmation of payment of the participation fee;
  • submission of the film according to the instructions provided by the organizers; and
  • submission of signed parental consent forms for participants under 18.

4. Selection Process & Awards

All eligible submissions will undergo a pre-selection process conducted by three evaluators appointed by the festival organizers. The pre-selection will use the same core criteria applied during the final judging process.

Selected films will be screened during the official Zoom In Film Fest event and evaluated by a jury comprising professionals and individuals with relevant experience in film and media.

Films will be assessed according to criteria including:

Originality • Screenplay • Directing • Acting • Cinematography • Sound • Editing • Set Design • Creativity • Narrative Structure

Prizes will be awarded for 1st Place, 2nd Place, and 3rd Place.

Winners will receive diplomas, and additional prizes—such as filmmaking equipment, workshops, courses, or other professional development opportunities—may be awarded depending on the partnerships and resources available for the respective festival edition.

The winners will be announced during the official Zoom In Film Fest Awards Gala.
