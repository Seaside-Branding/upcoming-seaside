Our Partners

ZIFF is organised by young people who believe that creativity can make a difference. Our team brings together students and volunteers from different schools, along with filmmakers, heritage professionals and people involved in the “Adoptă o Casă” programme in Roșia Montană.
Through film and the work we do around the festival, we want to help more people discover the history, people and places of Roșia Montană and understand why they are worth preserving. We hope to turn curiosity into action and play our part in helping this heritage have a future.

Join Us!

ARA ASSOCIATION

ZIFF works with the ARA Association and the “Adoptă o Casă” programme to support the preservation of Roșia Montană’s built heritage.
If you have questions about specific restoration projects, such as the Lapidarium at the Mining Museum, the Casina, or about traditional building techniques and crafts, you can contact our partners directly:
