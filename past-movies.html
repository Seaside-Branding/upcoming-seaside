Finish line

"Every day the same monotony. There’s no more difference in my routine..."

An introspective journey through solitude, overthinking, and the endless urge to escape your own thoughts. Shot on location in cold, late-night streets, this short film explores the weight of modern isolation and the noise inside our heads.

🏆 Winner — ZIFF (Zoom-In Film Festival) 2026

I Can't help myself

I Can’t Help Myself is an intimate psychological short film that explores the fragile line between solitude, indulgence, and control.

When a young woman returns home to an empty apartment, a quiet evening quickly shifts into a visceral sensory experience. Under glowing lights and captivating music, an uncontrollable urge takes over—leading to a bittersweet moment of raw truth and vulnerability.

Directed by Mara Copil
Starring Sofia Vilau

Fairy Tales in Real Life 📖✨
