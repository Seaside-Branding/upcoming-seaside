Press Room: Zoom in Film Festival 2027



Welcome to the official press hub for the Zoom in Film Festival (ZIFF) 2027. This edition represents an ambitious evolution of our mission to synthesize youth-led creative initiative with professional heritage conservation. ZIFF 2027 is more than a cinematic event; it is a vital dialogue between the urban transition of Bucharest and the rural continuity of Roșia Montană.

Get started

The ZIFF Mission

The festival serves as the public-facing engine of a sophisticated philanthropic ecosystem dedicated to supporting the "Adopta O Casa" program. Our philosophy, "Zoom In," directs the cinematic lens toward endangered history to act as a catalyst for its physical restoration. By showcasing "engaged cinema"—powerful digital narratives that reflect values of creativity, innovation, and social responsibility—we aim to translate the "dust and stones" of the past into a restored future.

2027 Festival Highlights

Authentic Venues: Moving beyond a single location, the 2027 edition inhabits authentic and inspiring cinema locations across Bucharest, creating an intimate environment for deep reflection on heritage and documentary realism [411, Conversation History].

Curatorial Focus: The program prioritizes "slow cinema" and documentary realism, featuring works that capture the pulse of a new generation of storytellers in a turbulent world.

Direct Heritage Impact: Festival advocacy directly supports high-priority restoration projects managed by the ARA Association. Following the successful 2026 interventions at the Mining Museum’s Lapidarium and the Reformed Church, the 2027 campaign continues to fund traditional crafts and sustainable employment for local craftsmen.

The Roșia Montană Connection
Roșia Montană is a site of exceptional historical value, featuring over 2,000 years of mining history and the largest number of heritage monuments in any Romanian village. The ZIFF initiative serves as a primary pipeline for the Heritage Summer Campaigns, where cinematic engagement in the city transitions into hands-on restoration in the mountains. Participants and supporters contribute to the "scientific endeavor" of saving structures using traditional materials like lime-sand mortar and wooden shingles.

Media Assets & Accreditation
